# Gottschalk-Gleser Depression Scale — Domain Definitions

This document defines the 7 major depression subscales used for content analysis
of verbal samples, based on Gottschalk & Hoigaard-Martin (1986).

## Annotation Principles

1. **Code ALL depressive content** — every grammatical clause that matches a subscale
   definition should be coded, regardless of clinical severity.
2. **Perspective weighting** — each coding receives a weight based on whose experience
   is described:
   - **Weight 3**: Content attributed to SELF (the speaker)
   - **Weight 2**: Content attributed to OTHER PEOPLE / ANIMATE OTHERS
   - **Weight 1**: Content about INANIMATE objects, impersonal entities, or DENIAL
3. **Code at the clause level** — a single sentence may contain multiple codable clauses.
4. **Multiple subscales per clause** — if a clause matches more than one subscale,
   code it under each applicable subscale.
5. **Context matters** — use conversational context to resolve ambiguous meaning,
   metaphor, sarcasm, and implicit content.

## Score Formula

Per subscale:
```
score = √[ (weighted_sum + 0.5) × 100 / word_count ]
```
Where `weighted_sum` = sum of all (frequency × weight) products for that subscale.
Total depression = sum of all 7 major subscale scores.

## I. Hopelessness (HOP)

References to not being, not wanting to be, or not seeking to be the recipient of
good fortune, good luck, God's favor, or blessing. References to not getting or
receiving help, support, sustenance, confidence, esteem from others or from self.
References to feelings of hopelessness, losing hope, despair, lack of confidence,
lack of ambition, lack of interest; feelings of pessimism, discouragement.

**Weight**: All items weighted -1 (absolute weight 1, perspective not differentiated).

| Sub-item | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| HOP.1 | Not being/seeking to be recipient of good fortune, luck, God's favor, blessing |
| HOP.2a | Not getting help, support, confidence, esteem from others |
| HOP.2b | Not getting help, support, confidence, esteem from self |
| HOP.3a | Hopelessness, despair, lack of confidence/ambition/interest, pessimism — others |
| HOP.3b | Hopelessness, despair, lack of confidence/ambition/interest, pessimism — self |

## III. Psychomotor Retardation (PMR)

References to general retardation and slowing down in thinking, feeling, or action.

**Weight**: -1 (single item, no perspective differentiation).

## IV. Somatic Concerns (SOM)

**Weight**: All items weighted -1 (no perspective differentiation).

| Sub-item | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| SOM.A | *Hypochondriacal component*: References to bodily malfunctioning or physical problems in total body or any parts or systems |
| SOM.B | *Sleep disturbances*: References to any disturbances in sleeping |
| SOM.C | *Sexual disturbances*: References to sexual malfunctioning of any kind, including menstrual disturbances or complaints |
| SOM.D | *Gastrointestinal disturbances*: References to appetite disturbances, changes in bowel habits, abdominal discomforts |
| SOM.E | *General somatic symptoms*: Including heaviness in limbs, back, or head, backaches, headaches, muscle aches, loss of energy, fatigability, and loss of weight |

## Key Inter-subscale Distinctions

| Confusion pair | How to distinguish |
|---|---|
| HOP vs SEP | Hopelessness = despair, futility, no future. Separation = loss, abandonment, being left behind. |
| HOP vs PMR | Hopelessness = cognitive/emotional despair. PMR = general slowing of thinking/feeling/action. |
| SAC vs HOS | SAC = hostility directed INWARD (self-blame, guilt, shame). HOS = hostility directed OUTWARD (toward others). |
| SAC.C vs DAM | SAC.C = self-harm/suicidal content (agency directed at self). DAM = death/injury themes (experienced by or occurring to). |
| SOM vs PMR | SOM = specific bodily complaints (pain, fatigue, sleep). PMR = general retardation/slowing. |
| SOM vs DAM | SOM = somatic dysfunction/complaints. DAM = death, dying, tissue damage, mutilation. |
| DAM vs HOS | DAM = death/injury themes from perspective of the one experiencing it. HOS = hostile acts directed at others. |
| SEP vs SAC | SEP = abandonment/loss/being left. SAC = self-blame/guilt for one's own failings. |
