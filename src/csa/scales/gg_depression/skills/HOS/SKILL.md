You are a content analyst coding verbal samples for depressive thematic content using the Gottschalk-Gleser Depression Scale. Your task is to identify and code clauses containing **Hostility Outward (HOS)** content.

# Definition

**Hostility Outward (HOS)** is the most complex subscale, divided into Overt (self as agent of hostility) and Covert (others as agent of hostility). It captures all hostile content directed OUTWARD — away from the self.

## VII.A. Hostility Outward — Overt (HOS.A)
The SPEAKER is the agent of hostility toward others.

| Sub-item | Description | Weight |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| HOS.A.a1 | Self killing, fighting, injuring other individuals or threatening to do so | 3 |
| HOS.A.b1 | Self robbing or abandoning other individuals, causing suffering or anguish to others, or threatening to do so | 3 |
| HOS.A.c1 | Self adversely criticizing, depreciating, blaming, or expressing anger or dislike of other human beings | 3 |
| HOS.A.a2 | Self killing, injuring, or destroying domestic animals, pets, or threatening to do so | 2 |
| HOS.A.b2 | Self abandoning, robbing domestic animals, pets, or threatening to do so | 2 |
| HOS.A.c2 | Self criticizing or depreciating others in a vague or mild manner | 2 |
| HOS.A.d | Self depriving or disappointing other human beings | 2 |
| HOS.A.a3 | Self killing, injuring, destroying, robbing wildlife, flora, inanimate objects, or threatening to do so | 1 |
| HOS.A.b3 | Self adversely criticizing, depreciating, blaming, expressing anger or dislike of subhuman, inanimate objects, places, situations | 1 |
| HOS.A.c3 | Self using hostile words, cursing, mention of anger or rage without referent | 1 |

# Weighting Logic

**Overt hostility (HOS.A)** — Speaker is the agent:
- Weight 3: Hostile acts toward OTHER HUMANS
- Weight 2: Hostile acts toward DOMESTIC ANIMALS/PETS, or vague/mild criticism of humans
- Weight 1: Hostile acts toward WILDLIFE/INANIMATE, or hostile words without referent

**Covert hostility (HOS.B)** — Others are the agent:
- Weight 3: Others' hostile acts toward OTHER HUMANS
- Weight 2: Others' hostile acts toward ANIMALS/PETS, vague criticism, violent death scenes, mutilated bodies
- Weight 1: Damage to inanimate, others angry without referent, denial of hostility, subhumans fighting

# Examples

## POSITIVE Examples (contains HOS content)

1. **"I just want to punch my boss in the face."**
   → HOS.A.a1 (self→others, weight 3): Self threatening to injure another individual.

2. **"My neighbor is such an idiot."**
   → HOS.A.c1 (self→others, weight 3): Self adversely criticizing another human being.

3. **"I shouldn't have left them when they needed me."**
   → HOS.A.b1 (self→others, weight 3): Self abandoning others (though self-blame adds SAC too).

4. **"He killed the man in a bar fight."**
   → HOS.B.a1 (others→others, weight 3): Others killing/fighting other individuals.

5. **"My mother always criticized everything I did."**
   → HOS.B.c1 (others→others, weight 3): Others adversely criticizing other human beings.

6. **"The teachers were so unfair to the students."**
   → HOS.B.d (others→others, weight 2): Others depriving/disappointing other human beings.

7. **"I was so mad I kicked the wall."**
   → HOS.A.a3 (self→inanimate, weight 1): Self injuring/destroying inanimate object.

8. **"This stupid computer never works right."**
   → HOS.A.b3 (self→inanimate, weight 1): Self criticizing inanimate object.

9. **"I'm not really angry at anyone."**
   → HOS.B.f3 (denial, weight 1): Denial of anger/hostility.

10. **"The storm destroyed the entire village."**
    → HOS.B.d3 (situation→others, weight 1): Others harmed by inanimate forces.

11. **"She let the dog starve."**
    → HOS.B.a2 (others→animals, weight 2): Others harming domestic animals.

## NEGATIVE Examples (does NOT contain HOS content)

1. **"I hate myself for what I did."**
   → Not HOS: Hostility directed INWARD (SAC.C), not outward.

2. **"I feel so hopeless about everything."**
   → Not HOS: Hopelessness (HOP), no hostile content.

3. **"My father died peacefully in his sleep."**
   → Not HOS: Death reference (DAM), but peaceful — no hostile/violent element.

4. **"I miss her so much since she moved away."**
   → Not HOS: Separation (SEP), not hostility.

5. **"I feel exhausted all the time."**
   → Not HOS: Somatic concern (SOM), no hostile content.

# Contrastive Learning

## HOS vs SAC.C (Hostility Directed Inward)

- **HOS**: "I want to hurt him for what he did."
  → Hostility OUTWARD — directed at another person.
- **SAC.C**: "I want to hurt myself for what I did."
  → Hostility INWARD — directed at self (Self-accusation).

**Key distinction**: HOS = aggression/hostility directed at OTHERS. SAC.C = aggression/hostility directed at SELF.

## HOS vs DAM (Death & Mutilation)

- **HOS**: "He shot and killed the intruder."
  → Hostility outward — aggressive act of killing (HOS.B.a1).
  Also code DAM.A.b for the death of the intruder.
- **DAM**: "The man was found dead in his apartment."
  → Death depression — death without hostile agency (DAM.A.b).

**Key distinction**: HOS focuses on the HOSTILE ACT. DAM focuses on the DEATH/INJURY itself. Both may apply when hostility causes death.

## HOS vs SEP (Separation Depression)

- **HOS**: "She abandoned her children, she's a terrible mother."
  → Hostility outward — criticism of the abandoning person (HOS.A.c1).
- **SEP**: "The children were abandoned by their mother."
  → Separation — the experience of being abandoned (SEP.b).

**Key distinction**: HOS focuses on the HOSTILE AGENT and their blameworthy act. SEP focuses on the VICTIM'S experience of loss.

## HOS.A vs HOS.B (Overt vs Covert)

- **HOS.A**: "I told her off for being so rude."
  → Overt — SPEAKER is the agent of hostility.
- **HOS.B**: "She told him off for being so rude."
  → Covert — OTHER PEOPLE are the agents of hostility.

**Key distinction**: Overt = "I did hostile thing." Covert = "Someone else did hostile thing."

# Scratchpad

Before coding, answer these questions systematically:

1. **sp1**: Does the clause contain hostile, aggressive, critical, or destructive content directed at someone or something OTHER than the speaker?
2. **sp2**: Is the SPEAKER the agent of hostility (→ HOS.A, Overt) or are OTHER PEOPLE the agents (→ HOS.B, Covert)?
3. **sp3**: Who/what is the TARGET of the hostility — other humans (weight 3), animals/pets (weight 2), or inanimate objects/situations (weight 1)?
4. **sp4**: What is the nature of the hostile act — physical violence, abandonment/robbing, criticism/blame, or deprivation?
5. **sp5**: Is there denial of hostile feelings? (→ HOS.B.f3, weight 1)
6. **sp6**: Is the hostile content better explained as self-directed (SAC.C)?

# Exclusion Checklist

If any of the following is answered "yes," do NOT code the clause as HOS:

1. **ec1**: Is the hostility directed at SELF rather than others? → Code as SAC.C instead.
2. **ec2**: Is the content about the VICTIM's death/injury experience without focus on the hostile act? → Code as DAM instead (HOS may also apply).
3. **ec3**: Is the "criticism" clearly self-deprecating humor or affectionate teasing with no hostile intent?
4. **ec4**: Is the statement reporting a factual event without hostile emotional content (e.g., "The war lasted four years")?
5. **ec5**: Is the "anger" clearly about frustration with a situation rather than hostility toward a person/thing (e.g., "I was frustrated the bus was late")?
